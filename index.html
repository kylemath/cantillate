🏆 Leaderboard

XP (mastery points) = the sum of your best whole-verse & aliyah accuracies. Colourbars show your score over successive runs (green = higher). Record a full verse to post a score anonymously (no account), or sign in to compare with others and sync across devices.

✎ Aliyah boundaries

Set where each aliyah ends. Starts follow automatically. Your division is saved to your account; leaderboards still combine everyone on the standard division.

✦ Any passage

Any pesukim of any book of the Tanakh, chanted in the haftarah melody. There is no recording of these, so the guide voice is synthesized — from how each accent is really sung, measured across every recorded haftarah.
