Drag a tick in the waveform to move that word. Underlines mark words the aligner was least sure of (orange, then red) — those are worth hearing first.
Double-click a transition to break it in two: the word before it stops at the left edge, the word after starts at the right, and a false start, a cough or an aside caught in between is played by neither. Drag either edge to fit it to what you hear, Skip cuts to listen to the result, and double-click the cut again to close it back up.
